About

“

It's important to think about the interconnectedness between designers and thinkers, instead of trying to find a space you can occupy yourself.

— Virgil Abloh

Hi, I'm Emily — a UI/UX designer and researcher who believes good design is never made alone. I'm based in Vancouver, currently studying Design and Interactions at SFU, with specializations in copywriting, graphic design, UI/UX design, and content strategy.

I like to think of myself as a translator between ideas and experience: part strategist, part empath. The best copy and the best interfaces, to me, share one quality — they disappear. They let people move through a product without ever noticing the thinking that made it feel effortless.

Outside of design, you'll usually find me with a book in one hand and a sketchbook in the other, or down a rabbit hole learning about open-source design systems (yes, for fun).
